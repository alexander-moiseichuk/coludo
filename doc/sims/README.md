# Flight simulations

Closed-loop flight simulations of the Coludo glider — the *real* control code (`navigation` + `pid` +
`mixer` + `sequencer`) flown over the shared flight-dynamics model (`src/glider/sim_model.py`), in two
worlds that share the same physics: the host **virtual-flight** tool (CPython) and the on-board **HITL**
simulator (`tasks/hitl.py`, MicroPython). Each run emits a recorder capture rendered to interactive
plotly HTML + dependency-free SVG.

| Set | What was tested | Where it ran |
|---|---|---|
| [**TMS-7-basic**](TMS-7-basic/) | The original bank-to-turn glide on the TMS-7 airframe (F15): sensor-noise sweep (5–100 %), cross-wind sweep (0–12 m/s), and g16 spike-injection corner cases. The reference that showed the over-range → orbit fix (miss ~85 m). | **host** (`tools/virtual_flight.py`) |
| [**TMS-7-guarded_fins**](TMS-7-guarded_fins/) | The **g12 dynamic-pressure fin governor + boost control stage**, flown as a full matrix (noise 05–100, wind 00–12, corner spike/stress) **on the real ESP32-P4** for **both motors — F15-4 and E16-4**. Every trace (trajectory, fins, **real board vitals**) is device telemetry, collected via rshell-run / adb-take. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-memory_refactoring**](TMS-7-memory_refactoring/) | The **GC-off memory leak** after the Phase-3 hot-path allocation work (fixed-point PID + telemetry/`note` trims), measured from the `mem_free` slope over BOOSTING→DONE. A full on-board HITL capture set of the post-refactoring firmware over the corrected TMS-7 v2 weight matrix (E16/F15 × full 300 g / half 150 g glider; booster ejects at separation). Calm, 5 % noise, 25 Hz. Each flight has a 3D report (trajectory + attitude + fins + **real INA226 servo power** + **GC-off leak/OOM**), an SVG, and a shared movie. Real-flight control-path leak measured ~24 KB/s → **time-to-OOM ≈ 10 min** (F01 + F05), well past the 180 s gate. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-fixnums**](TMS-7-fixnums/) | The same weight matrix re-flown on the **fixed-point (`fixnum`) + gyro-D-term firmware** (centidegree integer PID/attitude/drivers, LSM6DSO32 rate as the PID D term, integer INA226). Behaviour is baseline-neutral (same trajectory, power, CPU); the HITL leak is flat (sim-dominated). The measured **real control-path leak is ~42 KB/s → time-to-OOM ~13 min** at 100 Hz (`pid.step` now 0 B/step; the residual is the deliberately-float airspeed `|accel|` chain), and each report adds a **gyro-rate panel**. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-guiding_refactoring**](TMS-7-guiding_refactoring/) | The weight matrix re-flown after the **structural refactoring** (plan roadmap #1–#3): the control law extracted to `guidance.py` (per-stage law table) + `governor.py` (airspeed cap + adaptive throttle), the **mixer fused into the servo write** (`bind`/`actuate`), and `virtual_flight` driving the real law. **Trajectory parity is exact** (apogees to the metre vs fixnums) while the real control-path leak drops to **~15 KB/s settled → time-to-OOM ~36 min** at 100 Hz (PID+mix+servo slice 112 → 32 B/step). Four reports and SVGs (movie regenerable via `flight_video.py`). | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-loiter**](TMS-7-loiter/) | The fly-long glide law before/after (**host** sim, quality-2 polar): the old racetrack's 184 m phase-luck swings vs the **loiter orbit + endgame spiral** landing **17–18 m from the centre, in-zone, both motors** — with time aloft 121–148 % of the polar ceiling throughout. 3D reports + SVGs + the movie; the on-board confirmation matrix completes it. | **host** (`virtual_flight`) |
| [**TMS-7-warm_reboot**](TMS-7-warm_reboot/) | Eight cases on the **measured v3 weights** flying the **fly-long trim law** (objectives ① time aloft ② in-zone ③ midpoint) on the worst-case quality-2 polar: glides +30…46 % vs pitch-0, and **the E16 reaches the zone for the first time** (13.5 m in-zone light / 77 m full); F15-light 16.6 m in-zone; the endgame variance (F15-full 109 m) is the baselined open item of the glide-energy program. Plus on-board **warm start** (breadcrumb → five-signal gate → restore, both flights recovered; outage cost is a ballistic-sim artifact) and the **CC-less spiral-landing fallback** (field-agent zone, full flights). Eight 3D reports/SVGs + the FHD 30 FPS movie with per-flight what-is-flying titles. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-attitude**](TMS-7-attitude/) | **Attitude REDUNDANCY** + loiter board provenance in one round (6 cases, v3 weights, quality-2). 4 nominal loiter cases confirm the tuned orbit + endgame spiral **on-device** — in-zone both motors both weights (miss 52–61 m); 2 drop cases **kill the BNO055 mid-glide** so the priority-1 complementary-filter backup (`tasks/attitude.py`, integer-CORDIC roll/pitch, yaw-rate-gated accel) flies the glide to a controlled landing (E16 **15 m in-zone**). Backup tracks truth to ~1° roll / ~0.5° pitch (`attitude_soak`). 6 reports/SVGs + the 30 FPS movie. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7-oo_landing**](TMS-7-oo_landing/) | The **`oo` two-lobe endgame** (the `guidance.Heading` enum + `auto` pattern selection, the Mission choosing `o`/`oo` from the zone aspect) flown across E16/F15 × full/half at **3 m/s wind**. A **documented negative result**: `oo` is stable (same-turn-sense lobes, no figure-8 divergence) and closer in still air, but overruns the 40 m strip + drifts on wind — **out of zone in 3 of 4** (F15-half in-zone at 62 m). The reliable in-zone endgame stays the single circle (`endgame_pattern: 'o'`). 4 reports/SVGs. | **board** (on-board HITL) |
| [**TMS-7_endgame_x3**](TMS-7_endgame_x3/) | The three endgame patterns **`o` / `ov` / `oo`** + the 3-tier `auto` selector (k<2→o, 2–6→ov, ≥6→oo) over the HPRC strip (k≈4.7), flown at **air-quality 2 (worst-case floor) and 5 (realistic)** via the `sim_model.trim_sink` / `VF_QUALITY` knob. The **converging `ov`** (leg-reversal reach ∝ altitude → collapses onto centre) now lands **in-zone at 22–27 m at q5 — closest of all three, beating `o` (60 m) and `oo` (74 m)** — but needs that sink budget: at the q2 floor it lacks the laps to converge (110 m) so `o` stays the floor-safe default. `ov` beating `oo` on the k≈4.7 strip pushed the `oo` tier out to k≥6. 3 reports + 3D overlay + plan SVG. | **host** (`virtual_flight`) |

| [**TMS-7-physics_refresh**](TMS-7-physics_refresh/) | The canonical matrix (E16/F15 × full/light, calm) re-flown after the sim gained the three things it was missing: an **airspeed drag polar** (sink ∝ the polar, min-sink at ~0.76×trim as on a real glider — calm-at-trim runs are unchanged, the *shape* was the gap), **gusts** (an OU disturbance, default 0 so earlier studies reproduce), and **fault injection** (`VF_FAULT=gnss@30`; a faulted pitot RAILS rather than going silent, the dangerous under-read). Two new results: the converging endgame **degrades gracefully under gusts** (27→39 m at 3 m/s 1-σ, still in-zone — its robustness margin was previously unknown), and an injected **pitot failure costs nothing** (27.0 m, identical to baseline) because the saturation guard falls back to the accel backbone, while losing **GNSS costs 720 m**. | **host** + **board** |
| [**TMS-7-phase5_refactor**](TMS-7-phase5_refactor/) | The canonical matrix re-flown **on the board** after the phase-5 refactor round (bus `bind()` extraction, `.mpy` deploy onto a wiped board, a CORDIC that is accurate at small magnitude, and the **simulated pitot**). Three of four cases land within **2 m** of the host quality-2 prediction and `f15_full` reproduces the previous board runs inside their own 0.5 m spread — the refactor is behaviour-neutral on the flight path. The new signature is the pitot finally being IN the loop: the governor tracks it to ~0.2 m/s (was a 14.1 m/s estimate with the real bench sensor gated out all flight), and the ±500 Pa **saturation guard is exercised on the board** for the first time. Also reconciles **TMS-7-loiter**, whose 17–18 m in-zone numbers predate the harness correction and do not survive (~100 m optimistic). Investigating the leak found the **OOM forecast was unusable** (271→155→119→None→362 s, and it arms a 200 ms mid-glide collect) and the rescue was firing on a **third redundant safety margin** — both fixed, pauses halved from 10 to 5 across the matrix with the heap never below 16 MB of ~30. | **board** (on-board HITL) |

Each subfolder's `README.md` has the per-scenario metric tables (miss / in-zone / max-from-pad /
duration), the findings, and the regenerate commands.
